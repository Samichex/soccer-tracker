About

This site tracks NCAA Division I men's soccer scores, schedules, and stats.

Data source

Scores, schedules, and box scores come from the ncaa-api project, an independent feed built on NCAA.com's own data. This site isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the NCAA.

How often it updates

Games in the next few days are refreshed every 30 minutes, and you can hit “sync now” for an immediate check. Further-out schedule dates update once a day, since they rarely change until much closer to kickoff.

A note on accuracy

Occasionally the upstream feed has a garbled name or an odd stat line — when that happens, this site does its best to clean it up automatically rather than show clearly bad data. If something looks off, it's likely a quirk in the source feed rather than this site.
